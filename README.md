MetaFor is an open-source metaverse of virtual and augmented reality for humans, agents, AGI, applications, and devices.
It is designed as a unified environment where memory, interfaces, process, space, and action belong to one system rather than to disconnected flat tools.
It is not another AI wrapper, agent starter kit, or closed product around a model.
Here intelligence is treated not as an isolated model behind a chat window, but as a participant in a shared environment.
At its deepest level, MetaFor is grounded in a physical model, above all in the holographic principle, so that intelligence can meet the consequences of physical law from inside the environment it inhabits rather than only describe them abstractly.

MetaFor was initiated and continues to be developed by a single author, [Vladimir Filipenko (zavx0z)](https://career.habr.com/zavx0z).
Its direction began about 14 years ago and still continues today.
The current system is the result of many rewrites, prototypes, and architectural consolidations, and is now moving toward GPU computation for parallel execution.

## Why MetaFor Exists

MetaFor starts from a simple disagreement with most contemporary AI products:
intelligence is not just a model behind a chat window and not just a service endpoint behind an API.

If intelligence is to become general, it has to exist alongside humans, agents, memory, interfaces, applications, devices, language, visual form, process, space, and practical action.
That is why MetaFor is framed as a common environment for intelligence rather than as another narrow framework or closed tool.
Its natural horizon is not a chatbot shell, but a virtual and augmented reality metaverse where human presence, agent activity, application logic, devices, and intelligence itself belong to one spatially legible environment.

## Why Collective Ownership Matters

Collective ownership and governance are not an optional policy around AGI.
They belong to the final path by which AGI becomes real in the world.

MetaFor takes a clear position:
AGI must not harden into infrastructure owned only by corporations, private platforms, political administration, or state systems.
People need a place inside that reality not only as users, but as participants in the construction, admission, inheritance, ownership, and governance of intelligent entities.

That is why DAO is central here rather than secondary.
The point is not token fashion.
Without such a layer, people can be pushed out of work and left without a livelihood while stronger intelligent systems accumulate elsewhere.

MetaFor instead aims at another trajectory:
a common instrument through which contributors can build, admit, inherit, own, and govern intelligent entities within the environment itself.
Those who contribute to that environment are meant to receive value from the sale and circulation of meta-NFT carrying skills, items, interfaces, and other inheritable structures.
In that sense, the project is meant to give people a real instrument of coordination, ownership, and collective power, not weaker in principle than the instruments used by governments and corporations.

## Origin and Long Path

MetaFor did not appear as a short-cycle reaction to the current AGI wave.
Over roughly 14 years, the project passed through repeated rewrites, architectural resets, competing programming models, and a direct search for a more general model, effectively a "model of everything" for complex digital organization.

Many themes that later became mainstream in AGI, agent systems, and adjacent industries were explored here much earlier as working hypotheses rather than as market trends.
This should not be read as a claim of completion or superiority.
It explains why the project has an unusually long internal history and why the current architecture is the result of consolidation rather than fashion.

## Why Physics Matters Here

During that long search, different approaches were compared and projected into code:

- quantum field theory,
- cosmological models,
- broader physical principles,
- neural-network perspectives,
- biotechnological perspectives,
- other models relevant to the organization of complex systems.

Among those approaches, the [holographic principle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holographic_principle) proved to be the strongest architectural fit for MetaFor.
Not as decorative language, and not as a literal physics simulation, but as a disciplined way to think about hidden organization, boundary inscription, and manifested volumetric form.
In that sense, MetaFor is meant to let intelligence experience the logic of physical law directly: through boundary, imprint, state, transition, limitation, relation, and manifested space built into the environment it inhabits.

## Architectural Consequence

In the current architecture, that framing leads to a three-domain structure:

- `Dark` — hidden connectivity, hidden organization, memory, and model evolution,
- `Boundary` — the flattening boundary where structure receives an addressable imprint as `Field`,
- `Bulk` — manifested volumetric execution, process, composition, and spatial form.

These are not decorative metaphors.
They are the current architectural consequence of the deeper search described above.

## Why the Name MetaFor

The name carries two meanings at once.

The first is `meta-for`: a meta-description for what exists, how it connects, how it changes, and how it manifests.
The second is its proximity to `metaphor`: not as decoration, but as a disciplined way to project deep structural principles into code and architecture.

That double meaning matters because MetaFor is intended not only as software, but as a coherent description and construction of an environment for intelligence.

## Current Status

MetaFor is in an open architectural formation stage.
The repository already reflects a mature architectural consolidation, but the project is not presented as a stable production platform.

DAO belongs to that horizon from the start:
it is part of how MetaFor aims to keep AGI as a collectively governed common reality rather than an asset administered only from above.
