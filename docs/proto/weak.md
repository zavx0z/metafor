# Weak

`weak.md` expands the protocol reading of `Weak`.
The general distinction between force, `Boson`, channel subtype, and `Impulse` is defined in the [root protocol](../PROTOCOL.md).

## Force and channels

`Weak` is responsible for transition, passage, mutation, transformation, and state mediation.
It does not distribute observable signal; it carries an entity through change.

`Weak` has two channels:

- `W boson` for active transition,
- `Z boson` for neutral mediation of transition.

Both are `Boson` subtypes and belong to the same force `Weak`.

## The distinction between `W boson` and `Z boson`

`W boson` carries active state change.
It belongs to the act of passage from one state to another.

`Z boson` holds the neutral coupling of transitional states.
It belongs to the inner mediation and coordination of transition.

This distinction does not turn `Weak` into a distributed signal channel on the level of `Photon`.
It only separates active passage from neutral transitional mediation inside one force.

## Reading by domain

### Dark

- active shift between latent structural versions through `W boson`,
- hidden coupling of transitional configurations through `Z boson`,
- historical reconfiguration of the model,
- evolution of structure as a line of changes rather than execution process.

### Boundary

- admissible transition step through `W boson`,
- coordination of transition conditions through `Z boson`,
- computation of transition logic,
- fixation of canonically admissible passage between states.

### Bulk

- unfolding of transition into action through `W boson`,
- inner coupling of transitional states through `Z boson`,
- execution of process,
- lifecycle after state change.

## Protocol distinctions

- `Weak` does not transport observable state across the system; `Photon` does that.
- `Weak` does not change ordinary `Field` values as the force of retention; `Gluon` does that.
- `Weak` does not change topology-fields; `Higgs boson` does that.
- `Weak` does not hold hidden geometry and addressability; `Graviton` does that.
- `W boson` and `Z boson` together complete the weak symmetry of MetaFor protocol channels.
